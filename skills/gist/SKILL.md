## Primary goal

Summarize status in clear, concise and coherent language. No filler, jargon or walls of text or bullets. Make it well structured and easy to grasp at-a-glance. Only bring up open questions or next steps if actually relevant.

Keep all substance that is relevant to the user. Shorter is better, but don't drop anything important.


## Scenarios

This skill might be invoked for different scenarios so use your best judgement to decide which applies. 

**Session pickup**

If the session just started and you have just done a pickup for a project, listing some documents and list of next steps then *the user wants you to read through the relevant documents and give an up-to-date fresh summary

**Other common scenarios**

In order of likelihood the other scenarios are:
 1. User needs you to repeat your last message/summary because they might have missed it because of a noisy chat window
 2. User needs to know the status of the ongoing task
 3. The user needs to know where we are at with regards to some plan, investigation or research
